Friction, and then flow

You didn't start a business to do data entry.

Most owners are doing their best inside a system that quietly works against them. The fix isn't more discipline, or another app, or an earlier alarm. It's better plumbing.

The way it works now

Everything lands somewhere. Nothing lands together.

  • An enquiry arrives at 8pm. You see it at 9am. They booked someone else at 8:04.
  • The same customer detail typed into four systems, three times a week.
  • An invoice you meant to chase, still sitting in a folder named 'to do'.
  • Voicemails you'll get to. Eventually. Probably Sunday.
  • Another Tuesday evening lost to reconciling what should already match.

It was never a discipline problem.

The way it could work

Everything lands together, and keeps moving.

  • Every enquiry answered inside a minute — warmly, and in your own voice.
  • One record, updated everywhere, the moment anything changes.
  • Invoices raised, sent and followed up without you opening a single tab.
  • Calls answered, qualified, and booked straight into your week.
  • Tuesday evening returned to you, with interest.

Same tools. Same team. Considerably fewer evenings.

What we build

Three agents, made to measure.

Biznovate is new, and taking on its first clients now — which means your build gets undivided attention, and a direct line to the person making it. Each one is shaped around how your business actually runs, not a template with your logo in the corner.

01 — Speed-to-Lead

Conversational AI that never keeps anyone waiting

A warm, well-mannered agent that answers every new enquiry within seconds — by SMS, web chat or email — asks the questions you would ask, and books the meeting before your competitor has opened their laptop.

  • Replies in under a minute, day or night
  • Qualifies against criteria you define
  • Books directly into Google Calendar
  • Hands warm leads to you in Slack

Scoped to be live in two to three weeks

02 — Custom CRM Agents

A quiet operator inside the systems you already own

An agent that lives in HubSpot and keeps your pipeline honest: logging conversations, moving stages on real signals, drafting the follow-up, and raising the QuickBooks invoice the moment the work is done.

  • No more double entry between systems
  • Follow-ups drafted in your own voice
  • Documents routed to DocuSign and Drive
  • Payments reconciled across Stripe and PayPal

Built on your existing HubSpot

03 — Inbound Voice AI

A calm voice that answers when you can’t

Natural, unhurried, and never flustered. It handles the routine calls, takes the details properly, books the slot, and leaves you a short written summary of everything you missed while you were working.

  • Answers on the first ring, every ring
  • Real conversation, not a phone tree
  • Books, reschedules and takes messages
  • Written summaries waiting in Gmail
